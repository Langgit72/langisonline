Assignment Description

The most useful assignment for me thus far is Homework 5, which consisted of heat sink analysis and lump capacatiance analysis. I think that Problem 1 of Problem Set 5 demonstrates clearly demonstrates the effective of fin systems. Through calculating how adding pin fins drastically reduces the thermal resistance of the system was demonstrative. Because the fins are thermally in parallel with the unfinned portion of the system, the overall thermal resistance is reduced. This is a powerful concept that I will carry forward in my future work. I also found the lump capacitance analysis to be very useful, as it is a powerful tool for analyzing transient thermal systems. The concept of thermal time constant and how it relates to the response of a system to a step change in temperature was very enlightening. Overall, Homework 5 was a very useful assignment that helped me to understand important concepts in heat transfer and thermal analysis. It also showed how practical the concept of thermal resistance. It allows you to easily calculate the temperature drop across a system and how it changes with different given the thermal resistance of the system. Given a constant heat flux.

The second question in homework 5 was also very useful, as it shows how to extend course concepts. In class we derived a temperature given the lumped capacitance model given an initial temperature and a convection environment. Whereas in the homework we instead had an initial temperature equal to the temperature of the convection environment and a constant energy source of electric power dissipated in the system. So while the fundemental concepts are the same, the ODE is slightly different and the solution is different. This was a very useful exercise in showing how to extend course concepts to new problems.
